## The Secret to Great Outputs: The Universal Prompt Formula

Even with the correct name, beginners sometimes struggle to get the AI to do exactly what they want. If you are starting out, remember this universal prompt formula:

> **【Who is doing what action】+【In what environment】+【How is the shot filmed】**

Keep your descriptions precise, add speed words (like "slowly" or "suddenly"), and avoid writing long, confusing essays.
