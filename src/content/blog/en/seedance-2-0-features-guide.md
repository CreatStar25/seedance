### 3. Multi-Image Consistency (The Game Changer)

Perhaps the most powerful feature of **Seedance 2.0** is its ability to keep characters consistent.

By uploading 3 to 6 images of the same subject from different angles, **Seedance** "memorizes" the character's features. This allows you to generate a storyboard or a series of clips where the main character looks exactly the same in every shot—a massive improvement for visual storytelling and brand consistency.

## How to Write the Perfect Prompt for Seedance

Getting started with **Seedance 2.0** is easy, but mastering the prompt will give you the best results. You don't need to write a novel; just follow this universal formula used by pro creators:

> **The Formula:** [Who is doing what] + [In what environment] + [How is the shot filmed]

**Example:**
* *"A girl in a white dress slowly turns around (Action), standing in a sun-drenched garden with blooming roses (Environment), the camera pushes in slowly from a low angle (Camera Movement)."*

**Pro Tips for Seedance Users:**
1.  **Use Speed Words:** Words like "slowly," "suddenly," or "rapidly" help the AI understand the rhythm of the video.
2.  **Be Specific:** Instead of "a nice place," try "a cyberpunk city street at night with neon lights."
3.  **Start Simple:** Don't aim for a full movie on your first try. Generate 10-20 clips to get a feel for the tool.
