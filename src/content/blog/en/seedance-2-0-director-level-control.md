Early AI video felt like **gacha**: same prompt, many rolls until something usable. **Seedance 2.0** pushes **controllable narrative**—multimodal references, shot-style instructions, and finer motion/light constraints—so you steer like a **director**, not a gambler. This **Seedance tutorial** lens covers **Seedance prompts** and how **Seedance news** updates should change your flow.

![Seedance 2.0 director-level control](https://img.seedance22.com/aiweb/seedance22-blog-2.png)

## Gacha pain: what was missing

Text-to-video improved semantics but **character, scene, and camera** still drifted across shots. Users had to:

- Stretch prompts without nailing one frame’s composition;  
- Reroll heavily;  
- Chain separate audio tools—lips and beat often misaligned.

**Seedance 2.0** uses **references + instructions** to shrink variance—the core of a serious **Seedance tutorial**: **lock references, then write the story**.

## Director-level control: four layers

| Layer | Practice | Seedance prompts |
|--------|-----------|------------------|
| Character & look | Turnarounds, **@** binding | “Match reference” traits |
| Scene & light | Env refs, palette, time of day | Light direction, mood |
| Camera & pace | Push/pull, shot size, duration | Shot vocabulary (CU, MS, follow) |
| Sound & dialogue | Ref audio, mood, pace | Lines + emotion for AV sync |

Use this as a **Seedance tutorial** checklist to cut useless rerolls.

## Multi-reference workflow: storyboard to cut

Common advanced steps:

1. **Grid / storyboard**: keyframes or sketches constrain plot.  
2. **Video reference**: short clip; prompt says “reference this motion” or rhythm.  
3. **Iterate**: fix character and scene first, then one shot’s motion and transitions.

When **Seedance news** changes ref limits, **@** syntax, or templates, update your pipeline.

## Seedance prompt tips

- **Structure before polish**: who, where, what; then camera and mood.  
- **Explicit binding**: **@** for identity, not vague “inspired by”.  
- **Avoid clashes**: static tripod vs. violent handheld unless intentional.  
- **Policy**: sensitive or real-person topics per current rules—see **Seedance news**.

## Summary

From gacha to direction means **trading randomness for explainable constraints**. **Seedance 2.0** makes **Seedance tutorial** content center on **storyboard → references → prompt → iterate**; track **Seedance prompts** best practices and **Seedance news** for model and product shifts.
