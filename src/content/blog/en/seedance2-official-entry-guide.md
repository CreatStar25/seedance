## What is Seedance 2.0?

**Seedance 2.0** adopts a unified multimodal audio-video joint generation architecture, supporting four types of input modalities: text, images, audio, and video. It integrates the industry's most comprehensive multimodal content reference and editing capabilities to date. Compared to version 1.5, it shows significant improvements in generation quality, usability in complex interactions and motion scenes, physical accuracy, and controllability, making it more suitable for industrial-grade creative scenarios.

Key highlights include: higher usability and motion stability in complex scenes; support for mixed inputs of up to 9 images, 3 video clips, 3 audio clips, and natural language instructions; improved instruction following and consistency, supporting controllable video extension and editing; support for 15-second high-quality multi-shot audio-video output and dual-channel audio. **Seedance 2.0** has been integrated into platforms such as Jimeng AI and Doubao, and can be directly experienced through the following entry points.
