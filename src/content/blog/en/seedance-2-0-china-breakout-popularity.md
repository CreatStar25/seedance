## Seedance tutorial: beginner workflow

1. **Define the deliverable**: ad, narrative short, product demo, or talking head—decide refs and shots.  
2. **Prepare assets**: keyframes, style refs, optional motion and short audio (within limits).  
3. **Write Seedance prompts**: story and shots first, then light and rhythm; **@** where binding matters.  
4. **Iterate small**: short duration, one scene first; then expand.  
5. **Post**: export and tweak in your editor if needed.
