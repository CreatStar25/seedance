## What “leading” often means

| Lens | What users feel |
|------|------------------|
| Consistency | Character and scene hold across shots |
| Control | References + instructions match intent |
| Audio-video | Dialogue, rhythm, and picture align |
| Iteration | Under policy, cost of retries stays manageable |

Different lists stress different things; in a **Seedance tutorial**, state the **use case** (ads, drama, social) and avoid absolute slogans.

## Seedance tutorial: turn a strong model into outcomes

1. **Align the job**: define success (conversion, retention, brand).  
2. **Front-load assets**: turnarounds, boards, optional motion refs.  
3. **Seedance prompts**: clear story chain; **@** on key refs; avoid contradictory one-liners.  
4. **Evaluate**: fixed test set (same refs + prompts) across versions, not one lucky clip.

## Stay with Seedance news

Frontend and model limits (refs, duration, resolution) move fast.**Seedance news** should trigger **tutorial** refreshes so screenshots and params don’t mislead.

## Responsibility

Stronger models need stronger discipline on copyright, likeness, and misinformation.**Seedance 2.0** is for serious creative work; bake compliance into **Seedance prompt** and publishing checks.
