## 2. Two Modes

- **First & Last Frame**: Upload a first frame (or first and last frames) + a prompt, and AI generates the middle content. Ideal for getting started with **Seedance 2.0**.
- **All-in-One Reference**: Upload multiple images/videos/audio files. Use **@material_name** in the prompt to specify their purpose (e.g., @video1 for camera movement reference, @audio1 for background music). Suitable for character consistency and director-level control.

## 3. Prompts and @ Syntax

- Clearly describe: subject, environment, action, camera movement, style.
- Use @ to indicate the role of each uploaded material, e.g., "@image1 as the first frame", "@video1 reference this camera movement".
- For pure text-to-video, just input text. For stable control, it's recommended to add image or video references.
